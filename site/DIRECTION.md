# CASE landing page: direction

**Visual thesis.** A notes page that taps you on the shoulder. The whole site reads like one quiet CASE document on white paper (zinc neutrals, one blue), and the single loud moment is the hero: a live meeting that goes in circles until the card drops in, only on your side.

**Hero focal asset.** An HTML recreation of the CASE window (sidebar, meeting document, live dock) driven by one GSAP timeline: the transcript types the same question three ways, the level meter breathes, then the "Going in circles" card lands. No screenshot. Static first frame and no-JS state are the finished frame (card visible).

**Type.** Newsreader for display (400, tight), DM Sans for everything else, JetBrains Mono only inside the product recreation (timestamps, shortcut keys), as the app itself does. Scale: 13 / 15 / 17 / 22 / 32 / 56 / clamp display.

**Color.** background #ffffff, sidebar #f7f7f8, surface #f4f4f5, text #09090b, muted #52525b, line #09090b14, accent oklch(0.55 0.19 255). Dark mode follows the OS with the app's dark tokens.

**Sections.** Nav. Hero (headline + live window). Four reasons it speaks up (the real nudge kinds as the real card). Stays on your machine (local transcription, me/them, provider choice, platform table, honest about unverified platforms). Meetings as threads (rolling summary document). One shortcut. Watch the 20-second film (the project's own render). Download. Footer.

**Motion.** GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger. Lenis as the only smooth-scroll engine (Locomotive v5 wraps Lenis; Lenis alone is smaller and ties to ScrollTrigger with one ticker). Headings reveal word by word with a small stagger, once, on enter. One scrubbed sequence: none; the nudge section uses sequential, non-scrubbed reveals. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables Lenis and every timeline and renders final states.

**Three.js.** No. Nothing in the brief needs depth or displacement; a shader would be noise on a notes page.

**Assets and provenance.** `public/case-mark.svg` from `src/assets/brand/`. `public/media/case-launch.mp4` and poster from `brag-output/` (project's own render, fictional transcript). Interface symbols: Solar via Iconify, subset generated by `scripts/icons.mjs`. No avatars, testimonials, or logos: no evidence exists (PRODUCT.md), so none are shown.

**Skill.** frontend-design (installed), applied with DESIGN.md tokens as the brief.
